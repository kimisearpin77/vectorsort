Vector or Scalar?

Sort each physical quantity into the correct category.

Scalar Quantity

A quantity that is fully described by its magnitude (size or amount) and unit alone. It has no direction.

Example: a temperature of 25 °C, or a mass of 3 kg.

Vector Quantity

A quantity that has both magnitude and direction, and combines using the rules of vector addition.

Example: a velocity of 20 m/s north, or a force of 10 N downward.

Directions: Drag each quantity into the Scalar or Vector box. On a tablet or keyboard, tap/press a card to select it, then tap/press a box. Click a placed card to send it back. When every card is sorted, press Check Answers.
